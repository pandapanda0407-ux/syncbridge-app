Why this one is custom

eBay gives sellers far more room to differ from each other than most marketplaces. Business policies, item specifics that vary by category, multi-variation listings and SKU schemes inherited from years of trading all differ shop to shop. A fixed connector has to assume one shape and breaks on the rest, so we scope yours against your real catalogue instead.

In practice that means a short call about how your listings are structured, a look at your catalogue, and a build mapped to it. You are not waiting on a roadmap.
